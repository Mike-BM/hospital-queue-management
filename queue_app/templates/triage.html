Triage Categorization Guide
Level 2: Emergency (Immediate)

Imminent life or limb threat. Cardiac, severe respiratory, massive trauma, stroke symptoms, uncontrolled bleeding.

Level 1: Urgent

Severe discomfort, high fever with lethargy, moderate fracture, acute pain needing prompt medical attention.

Level 0: Routine

Stable conditions, routine checkups, prescription refills, chronic care follow-ups. Processed via standard FIFO.
